# 🧠 Open Cognitive Alliance

> "Intelligence should be a human right, not a corporate subscription."

## 🌍 The Crisis

In 2026, human cognition is held hostage:
- 🏢 **Knowledge Monopoly**: AI locked behind $20/month subscriptions
- 👁️ **Privacy Invasion**: Your thoughts train corporate models  
- 🔌 **Cloud Dependency**: No internet = no intelligence
- 💸 **Digital Divide**: $1,500+ devices exclude billions

We reject this future.

## ⚡ Our Mission

**Open Cognitive Alliance (OCA)** unites engineers, activists, and researchers to build a world where:

1. **Cognitive Independence** - Every person owns their AI, running locally on their device
2. **Absolute Privacy** - Your data never leaves your hardware
3. **Zero Subscriptions** - Intelligence is a one-time right, not a rental
4. **True Sovereignty** - No corporation can shut down your mind

## 🛡️ The Open Independence Standard

Any device earning the **OCA Certified** badge must guarantee:

✅ Open architecture (RISC-V or equivalent)  
✅ Freely licensed models (OSAID compliant)  
✅ Offline operation (no internet required)  
✅ Local-only data processing  
✅ User-owned encrypted weights  
✅ Right to modify and repair
