# 🧠 Open Cognitive Alliance (OCA)

> *"Intelligence should be a human right, not a corporate subscription."*

## 🌍 The Crisis We Face
In 2026, human cognition is increasingly held hostage by a centralized monopoly:
- 🏢 **Knowledge Monopoly**: Advanced AI is locked behind $20/month subscriptions and API paywalls.
- 👁️ **Privacy Invasion**: Cloud-based models feed on users' private thoughts, files, and data.
- 🔌 **Cloud Dependency**: No internet connection = no intelligence. 
- 💸 **The Digital Divide**: High-end local AI requires $2,000+ hardware, excluding billions of students and citizens.

**We reject this future. We believe cognitive independence is a fundamental human right.**

---

## ⚡ Our Mission
The **Open Cognitive Alliance (OCA)** unites hardware engineers, AI researchers, privacy activists, and policymakers to build an ecosystem where:

1. **Cognitive Independence** is guaranteed: Every person owns their AI, running locally on their device.
2. **Absolute Privacy** is the default: User data never leaves the local hardware.
3. **Zero Subscriptions**: Intelligence is a one-time right, not a lifelong rental.
4. **True Sovereignty**: No corporation or government can remotely shut down or censor your personal AI.

---

## 🛡️ The Open Independence Standard
We are defining the global standard for what constitutes a truly "Independent AI Device". Any hardware or software earning the **OCA Certified** badge must guarantee:

- ✅ **Open Architecture** (e.g., RISC-V or equivalent open silicon)
- ✅ **Freely Licensed Models** (Compliant with the OSI Open Source AI Definition)
- ✅ **Offline-First Operation** (Zero internet requirement for core reasoning)
- ✅ **Local-Only Processing** (No telemetry or cloud-syncing of prompts)
- ✅ **User-Owned Weights** (Encrypted, local storage of personal adaptation layers)
- ✅ **Right to Repair & Modify** (No hardware-level lock-in or DRM)
